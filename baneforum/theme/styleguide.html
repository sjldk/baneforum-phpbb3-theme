Style sheet

Every part of the theme in its real markup. Use it to see that a change keeps the parts alike, in both themes.

Type scale

Banner 2.5rem, Inter 700

Title 1.5rem, h2

Heading 1.125rem, h3, and forum and topic titles

Body 0.95rem, 400. Strong is 600. There are only two weights.

Meta 0.8125rem: dates, counts, column labels.

Surfaces

Three surfaces: the page (white), the panel (this box) and the inset (tint), and nothing else.
